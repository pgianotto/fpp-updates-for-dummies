## New features

- **File Manager replaces MP3Gain with Normalize**, which evens out songs by perceived loudness (LUFS) (ShowPilotFPP). Settings follow files when renamed, copied or deleted.
- The in-page Listen/View player was merged, with follow-up fixes.
- Crash reports record CPU temperature and clock speed at the time of the crash.

## MultiSync and audio

- A remote that finished early resyncs instead of going dark (Stephen Ripaldi).
- A remote playing more than two audio channels keeps up with its master.
- "Media playback did not start" now reaches the UI.

## Updates

- The update check on page load is throttled and runs at low priority (Pat Delaney); only a successful check counts as up to date.

## Other fixes

- A presets file deleted while fppd runs stays deleted; Media Slot Status shows no garbage before playback starts.

## Upgrade note

If you used MP3Gain in File Manager, use Normalize instead. It works on loudness rather than peak level, so levels may sound slightly different.
