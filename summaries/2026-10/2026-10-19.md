# FPP Updates — October 19, 2026

*Unofficial, AI-written summary of the day's commits to FPP master. See the [commit log](https://github.com/FalconChristmas/fpp/commits/master) for the details.*

42 commits from Daniel Kulp, darylc and Pat Delaney: tighter light-to-sound sync, protection against a corrupted fppd build, and backup and MultiSync fixes.

## Audio sync

- **The lights now follow the sound to within about 15 ms**, instead of drifting 1–50 ms off.
- After a sound card dropout, audio realigns immediately instead of at the next song.

## Updates and builds

- FPP detects an fppd build zeroed out by a power loss and offers a rebuild, and builds are flushed to disk so it can't happen (darylc).
- Upgrade 156 repairs a password config that upgrade 155 broke.

## Backups

- File Copy leaves other mounts alone, has its own progress feed, stops if the drive can't mount, and explains refused mounts (darylc).

## MultiSync

- The first and last ticked remotes are no longer dropped from the sync list (Pat Delaney).
- FPP recognizes its own broadcast echo on every interface.

## Security

- The virtual display assets folder is served sandboxed; color settings are hex only.
- FPP's own pages change state with POST; remote actions don't follow redirects.
- Kiosks and older-player proxies keep working under the new request checks.

## Other fixes

- A BeagleBone's built-in clock is set from the cape's clock, not the other way round.
- A Pi PWM output keeps its pin after a reload; event triggers fire their Command Preset again.
- The image size limit scales with the board's RAM; `fppd -v` sets the volume correctly.
