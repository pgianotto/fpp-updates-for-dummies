# FPP Updates — October 28, 2026

*Unofficial, AI-written summary of the day's commits to FPP master. See the [commit log](https://github.com/FalconChristmas/fpp/commits/master) for the details.*

7 commits from darylc and Daniel Kulp, mostly scheduler fixes. One commit landed just after midnight on October 29.

## Scheduler

- **Repeating schedule entries now hand over cleanly.** Each repeat ends exactly where the next one begins, so a lower-priority entry no longer sneaks in for a second between repeats, and one late start no longer makes every later hour late. Schedule previews and the schedule data now show repeat ends at :00:00 instead of :59:59 (darylc).
- The first run of a repeating entry is now cut off at its End Time. Previously a 20:00–20:10 entry repeating every 30 minutes could run 30 minutes and hold off lower entries (darylc).
- The scheduler's date-range lookup no longer lists playlists that end exactly when the range starts, such as ones ending at midnight showing up on the next day (darylc).

## Outputs

- The PWM, Other outputs and DMX input pages now keep Save switched off until your saved settings have finished loading. Before, a quick Save (or one after a failed load) could overwrite your real setup with blank defaults (darylc).

## Troubleshooting and diagnostics

- **New display tools on the Video tab:** see what the connected display reports about itself (EDID), the video system's current state and, on Pi 0–3, how busy the display hardware is. Diagnostic Reports include them with serial numbers removed. The help for the "No space left on device" video warning now suggests encoding the video at the display's resolution (darylc, early October 29).
- The low-power/throttling advice now matches the warning that is actually showing (darylc).

## Stability fixes

- Stopping or restarting fppd no longer pulls resources out from under audio/video playback that is still winding down, which could cause errors or crashes right after stopping a track.

## Upgrade note

- If you have scripts or integrations that read schedule times, repeat end times now show as :00:00 rather than :59:59.
