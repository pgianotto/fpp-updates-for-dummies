# FPP Updates — October 28, 2026

*Unofficial, AI-written summary of the day's commits to FPP master. See the [commit log](https://github.com/FalconChristmas/fpp/commits/master) for the details.*

7 commits from darylc and Daniel Kulp, mostly scheduler fixes. One commit landed just after midnight on October 29.

## Scheduler

- **Repeating schedule entries now stop when they should.** Each repeat ends where the next one starts, and the first run of a repeating entry is cut off at its End Time instead of running past it (darylc).
- The scheduler's date-range lookup no longer lists playlists that end exactly when the range begins (darylc).

## Outputs

- The Outputs page waits until the PWM, Other and DMX outputs are fully loaded before letting you save, so a quick click can't save an incomplete setup (darylc).

## Troubleshooting and diagnostics

- **New display tools on the Video tab:** show the connected display's identity (EDID), the video system's current state, and, on Pi 0–3, how loaded the display hardware is. Diagnostic Reports include them with serial numbers removed (darylc, early October 29).
- Help for warning 31 ("No space left on device" when a video starts) no longer blames something else on the port. It now says to encode the video at the display's resolution (darylc).
- The low-power/throttling advice now matches the warning flag that is actually set (darylc).

## Stability fixes

- Shutting down or restarting fppd no longer pulls files out from under audio/video threads that are still winding down, which could cause errors or crashes right after stopping a track.
