# FPP Updates — October 28, 2026

*Unofficial, AI-written summary of the day's commits to FPP master. See the [commit log](https://github.com/FalconChristmas/fpp/commits/master) for the details.*

7 commits from darylc and Daniel Kulp, mostly scheduler fixes. One commit landed just after midnight on October 29.

## Scheduler

- **Repeating schedule entries now stop when they should.** Each repeat ends where the next one begins, and the first run of a repeating entry is cut off at its End Time instead of running past it (darylc).
- The scheduler's date-range lookup no longer lists playlists that end exactly when the range starts (darylc).

## Outputs

- The Outputs page now waits until the PWM, Other and DMX outputs are fully loaded before letting you save, so a quick click can't save an incomplete setup (darylc).

## Troubleshooting and diagnostics

- **New display tools on the Video tab:** see the connected display's identity (EDID), the video system's current state and, on Pi 0–3, how busy the display hardware is. Diagnostic Reports include them with serial numbers removed (darylc, early October 29).
- The low-power/throttling advice now matches the warning that is actually showing (darylc).

## Stability fixes

- Shutting down or restarting fppd no longer pulls files out from under audio/video work that is still winding down, which could cause errors or crashes right after stopping a track.
