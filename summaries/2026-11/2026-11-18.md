# FPP Updates — November 18, 2026

*Unofficial, AI-written summary of the day's commits to FPP master. See the [commit log](https://github.com/FalconChristmas/fpp/commits/master) for the details.*

35 commits, all but one from Daniel Kulp (one from Stafford Brunk). The bulk of the day is one big, connected effort: making songs and videos hand off to each other with no gap, no flicker, and no drift between the lights and the sound, plus the string of smaller bugs that surfaced while building it. A couple of commits landed late in the evening of November 17.

## Playback and playlists

- **Back-to-back songs and videos now play with no gap between them.** Previously, FPP stopped and restarted its audio stream at every song change, leaving 116-143ms of silence between songs and about 200ms of frozen picture between videos. The audio stream now carries straight through into the next track's timeline when nothing is running late, so the next song's first note lands right where the last one's final note ended.
- **A song's lights now hold through its last few notes instead of cutting off early, and the next song's lights no longer jump in before its music starts.** The lights used to stop as soon as the media file reported "done," even though a little sound was still queued up to play — now they run out exactly as long as it takes for that last bit of sound to actually reach the speaker. The same fix also stops a sequence from guessing the wrong length for variable-bitrate MP3s.
- **Lights now track the music far more precisely and consistently.** FPP previously estimated when a buffer of sound would reach the speaker; now it measures it directly from the audio hardware every cycle. Lights that used to drift 13-40ms off the sound, and reset that drift at a different value every time a show started, now stay matched to within a fraction of a millisecond, song after song.
- A cluster of related fixes keeps this new back-to-back playback solid under real-world conditions: a brief audio glitch (an "xrun") no longer leaves the lights permanently behind the sound for the rest of a song; a late-arriving chunk of audio no longer forces the next song to restart from silence; a seek or a remote resyncing at just the wrong instant no longer plays a track late or drops it; and a video whose sound starts a frame or two after its picture no longer throws off the timing of every song that follows it.
- Fixed a bug where two songs' "let the lights finish the last few notes" timers could collide and cancel each other, leaving a sequence running when it should have stopped.
- Fixed a bug where, right after one song ended, FPP could briefly mistake the start of the next song's audio for the tail end of the previous one — which could report a stale playback position to MultiSync remotes and cut the previous song's sequence short.
- An inserted playlist (e.g., "Insert Playlist After Current") now preps the next video of the playlist it's returning to, so that handoff is also gap-free instead of starting cold.

## Video and the on-screen display

- Fixed several bugs where videos sharing the same screen/output could glitch during a handoff: a stray frame from the outgoing video briefly showing at the wrong size, a frozen or garbled frame appearing for a moment, or the on-screen display getting stuck occupied — blocking the system console or a Virtual Matrix from reappearing — after a video with no picture, a decoder that locks up, or quick back-to-back changes.
- The video display now only closes itself after 400ms with nothing using it, rather than the instant the current video lets go, which was closing and reopening the console or Virtual Matrix needlessly during normal pauses between videos.
- The video decoder now hands its own picture frames straight to the display instead of making a copy of each one first, cutting unnecessary CPU/memory work on every frame.

## MultiSync and updates

- **MultiSync remotes now pre-load the master's next video ahead of time**, the same way the master itself already does, so remotes showing video no longer go blank or restart cold between videos.
- Remotes are now told which sequence is about to play a little earlier, so they have time to load it (especially a large file on slower hardware) before it's actually needed, instead of scrambling at the last second.

## Stability fixes

- Fixed a handful of race conditions around shutting FPP down — prepared videos and video displays that were still being built or torn down could get cleaned up out of order, in rare cases while fppd itself was exiting.

## Outputs and hardware

- **Fixed GPIO pin mapping on SanCloud BBE boards** where it previously broke because the expected `/sys/class/gpio` interface wasn't present (credit Stafford Brunk).
- **Fixed detection of some PocketBeagle2 boards that FPP previously refused to recognize** because the factory's board-ID programming was left incomplete; FPP now treats that as unprogrammed (as it already did for a blank header) and lets the on-board storage pick the right board template instead of giving up.

## Upgrade note

MultiSync gained a new, optional "Prepare" message remotes use to pre-load upcoming video. Older remotes simply ignore it, so mixed-version MultiSync setups keep working without changes.
