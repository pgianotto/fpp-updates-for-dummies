# FPP Updates — November 24, 2026

*Unofficial, AI-written summary of the day's commits to FPP master. See the [commit log](https://github.com/FalconChristmas/fpp/commits/master) for the details.*

4 commits from November 24: three from Daniel Kulp and one from darylc, covering a new MultiSync networking option, a video sync timing fix, and a System Stats display cleanup.

## MultiSync and updates

- Added a new advanced setting, `MultiSyncUnicastSubnets`, that limits "Send MultiSync to ALL KNOWN remotes via Unicast" to a chosen list of subnets or IP addresses. Previously this option sent to every remote FPP instance it discovered via mDNS, even ones on unrelated networks (a test bench, another VLAN). Leaving the setting blank keeps the old behavior; individually selected remotes and the separate Unicast Discovery IPs list aren't affected by it.

## Video and audio

- Fixed a bug where resyncing a video with a remote player could hold the picture back for up to a couple of seconds after the seek (as long as 1.85 seconds on a Pi 4), leaving video noticeably behind audio right after a resync.
- Fixed a rare false-positive warning from Falcon's internal overnight testing tools around video playback. This doesn't change how FPP behaves for anyone running a show.

## System and UI

- The "Unknown Plugins" row on the System Stats page no longer wraps into one unreadable wall of text when several plugins are involved. It now shows a short count (e.g. "2 installed, 3 since removed"), with each plugin's details listed on its own line when you expand it. (darylc)
