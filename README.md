# FPP Updates for Dummies (Plain English)

Daily, plain-English summaries of what changed in [Falcon Player (FPP)](https://github.com/FalconChristmas/fpp), written for people who run light shows rather than people who write the code.

> **Unofficial.** These summaries are written by an AI (Claude) from the public commit log of the FPP `master` branch. They are not official release notes, and they may miss nuance or get a detail wrong. When something matters for your show, check the linked commits or ask in the FPP community.

## How to follow along

- Click **Watch → Custom → Commits** (or just **Watch**) at the top of this page to get notified when a new day is added.
- Or subscribe to the feed in any RSS reader: `https://github.com/pgianotto/fpp-updates-for-dummies/commits/main.atom`

## Where things are

- [`summaries/`](summaries/) — one file per day in a folder per month, named `YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- The summary for a day covers every commit that landed on FPP's `master` branch that day (US Eastern time).
- Days with no commits have no file.

## A note on "master"

These summaries follow FPP's development branch. Changes here usually reach players in the next FPP release or when you update a player from git, not straight away on a stock image.
